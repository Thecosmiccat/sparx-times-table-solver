SPARX SOLVER PRO — FIRST TIME ON macOS
======================================

The app is NOT damaged. macOS blocks downloads that are not from the App Store.

FASTEST FIX
-----------
Double-click:  Open Sparx Solver Pro.command

That clears the block and opens the app.


OTHER OPTIONS
-------------
• Right-click Sparx Solver Pro.app → Open → Open

• Terminal (copy one block):

  xattr -cr ~/Downloads/Sparx\ Solver\ Pro.app
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/Sparx\ Solver\ Pro.app 2>/dev/null
  open ~/Downloads/Sparx\ Solver\ Pro.app

  (Use /Applications/... if you moved the app there.)


PERMISSIONS (REQUIRED)
----------------------
System Settings → Privacy & Security:

  • Screen Recording  → ON for Sparx Solver Pro
  • Accessibility     → ON for Sparx Solver Pro

Then quit and reopen the app.
